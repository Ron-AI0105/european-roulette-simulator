Winning bets return your stake plus the listed profit. A winning 10-chip number bet returns 360 chips; a winning 10-chip red bet returns 20.

Chips are deducted when placed. Clear Bets refunds bets while betting is open. Betting closes automatically after 20 seconds, followed by a 6-second spin and 5-second results period. Bets are removed after settlement and never repeated.

There are 37 pockets: 0–36. Zero is green, not red, black, odd, even, high, or low. When zero lands, all outside, dozen, and column bets lose. No La Partage or En Prison.

Columns cover 1,4,7…34; 2,5,8…35; and 3,6,9…36. Each spin is independent. This version supports the bet types listed above.

This preview is entirely offline: no real accounts, multiplayer, money, tracking, or network requests. Every browser has its own table. Real shared accounts and rounds require the separate server package.

Timings are configurable design defaults, not an industry standard. Pause freezes this preview's betting timer; you may still place or clear bets. Background browser suspension can delay offline rounds. Reloading starts a fresh game.
